How will AI reshape JavaScript development, frontend frameworks, and developer careers? In this XtremeJS 2026 Xperts Panel, Daniel Ostrovsky, Mario Peshev, Jeremy Davis, and Nick Taylor discuss the future of frontend frameworks, whether deep JavaScript knowledge still matters when AI can write much of the code, and how software engineers should adapt as AI transforms the development process.
